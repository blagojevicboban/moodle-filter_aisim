AI Simulations Viewer: Interactive Machine Learning for Moodle

Brings AI and machine learning concepts to life with interactive, client-side simulations inside Moodle courses, aligned with the AI for VET curriculum.

Features

  • Interactive Simulations: Real-time visualizations of Gradient Descent, Regression Overfitting, Confusion Matrices, Decision Boundaries, Clustering, and Perceptron & Neural Networks.
  • Zero Server Load: 100% browser-based (HTML5 Canvas/JS) — no expensive GPUs, cloud APIs, or external tokens needed.
  • Hands-on Learning: Students adjust hyperparameters (learning rate, epochs, decision thresholds, k-neighbors) and instantly observe effects on convergence and accuracy.
  • Multilingual: English, Serbian Latin, and Serbian Cyrillic.
  • GDPR Compliant: Implements Moodle's Null Privacy Provider.

Requirements

  • Supports Moodle 4.5 LTS and 5.0 - 5.3 (tested on all versions).
  • PHP 8.2+.
